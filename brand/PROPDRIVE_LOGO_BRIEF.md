# PropDrive — Logo Brief

A working brief for designing the **PropDrive** logo. Hand this to any designer or
to ChatGPT/DALL·E. PropDrive is the **platform/product** brand (the SaaS sold to
agents), not a brokerage. The logo represents the company behind the product.

---

## 1. What PropDrive is

A modern real-estate **lead-generation platform** for agents and small
brokerages: polished listing websites + a lead CRM + branding tools. Think
"Squarespace meets a real-estate CRM." Sold/licensed to realtors.

- **Name meaning:** *Prop* (property) + *Drive* (momentum, driving leads and
  sales forward; also evokes a street / "Drive" as in an address).
- **One-liner:** "The real estate lead platform built for agents."

## 2. Personality

Premium, trustworthy, modern proptech, but **warm** rather than cold/techy.
Editorial and confident, like a high-end real-estate brand, not a startup.

- **Is:** refined, calm, established, aspirational, clean.
- **Is not:** loud, neon, crypto-techy, cartoonish, corporate-cold.

## 3. Brand system (must match)

- **Colors (OKLCH-friendly hex):**
  - Ink (near-black, warm): `#1A1714`
  - Clay (primary accent, terracotta): `#B85C38`
  - Cream (warm off-white background): `#FAF7F2`
- **Type pairing the brand uses:** Fraunces (editorial display serif) + Geist
  (clean sans). A wordmark in a refined serif **or** a confident geometric sans
  both fit; avoid generic/Inter.
- **Aesthetic name:** "Coastal Luxe" — warm, sunlit, Southern-California luxury.

## 4. Deliverables

1. **Horizontal lockup** — mark + "PropDrive" wordmark (primary).
2. **Mark only** — square, works as a 512px app icon **and** a 16px favicon.
3. **Monochrome** — single-color ink version and single-color cream version
   (for dark backgrounds).
4. Must read cleanly on **cream** and on **ink** backgrounds.

## 5. Mark concept directions (pick or blend)

- **A. "P" monogram + pin.** A refined letter P whose bowl doubles as a
  location pin or rounded roofline. Ownable, literal-but-subtle.
- **B. House + forward motion.** A simple geometric house/roofline that leans or
  extends into a forward arrow/road, expressing "Drive" (momentum).
- **C. Abstract drive/route.** A clean upward route/road line resolving into a
  roofline or doorway. More abstract, more "platform."

Favor geometric construction on a tidy grid. It should survive being shrunk to a
favicon: one clear silhouette, no fine detail.

## 6. Hard nos (these make it look generic / off-brand)

- No literal **car** (Drive ≠ automobile).
- No generic clip-art house or an overused dropped map-pin on its own.
- No rainbow or neon **gradients**; no 3D bevels, no heavy drop shadows.
- No **purple/blue** "AI" palette; stay in the ink/clay/cream system.
- No `Inter` font. No gradient-filled text.

## 7. ChatGPT / DALL·E prompt (ready to paste)

> Design a minimal, premium logo for "PropDrive", a warm, high-end real-estate
> lead-generation software platform for agents. Aesthetic: "Coastal Luxe" —
> sunlit Southern-California luxury, editorial and calm. Palette strictly: deep
> warm near-black ink `#1A1714`, terracotta clay `#B85C38`, warm cream `#FAF7F2`.
> Create a clean geometric mark that fuses a simple house/roofline with a sense
> of forward momentum (the "Drive"), plus a refined wordmark "PropDrive" in an
> elegant serif or confident geometric sans. It must work as a tiny favicon and
> on both cream and dark backgrounds. Flat vector, no gradients, no 3D, no drop
> shadows, no literal car, no generic map-pin, no purple. Show the horizontal
> lockup and the mark on its own.

## 8. Practical notes

- Final asset should be delivered as **SVG** (scales perfectly; doubles as the
  favicon). Raster (PNG) only for previews/social.
- Keep at least one version that is a **single path/color** for stamping,
  watermarks, and tiny placements.
